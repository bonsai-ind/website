What is Coresift?+

Coresift is a clinical-grade infant intelligence system built by Inspirium Tech in Ahmedabad, India. It uses non-contact sensors to track your baby's breathing, temperature and vitals, translates cries into their likely cause, and connects you to a pediatrician when it matters. Nothing touches your baby.

How does it monitor a baby without touching them?+

Proprietary sensor fusion: a camera, infrared thermal imaging and acoustic AI working together. It reads micro-respiratory rhythms and vitals straight through swaddles and blankets. No socks, no straps, no wires.

Can it really tell why my baby is crying?+

Yes. AI-driven acoustic translation identifies the physiological cause of distress: hunger, fatigue or pain. Instead of another alarm, you get an answer.

Where does "36 extra hours of sleep a year" come from?+

Coresift predicts wake-ups before they happen and intervenes exactly when it matters. That adds up to nearly 45 minutes of extra, uninterrupted deep sleep every week, which is about 36 hours a year.

What is the 3:00 AM Clinical Bridge?+

A 24/7 connection to an on-call pediatrician. Instead of panicked descriptions over the phone, the doctor already has your baby's continuous biometric trends and acoustic logs in front of them. Guesswork becomes immediate, data-driven medical triage.

Is Coresift meant for hospitals too?+

Yes. We are running advanced clinical pilots now, and the system is built to clinical standards from day one, for NICUs as much as nurseries.
